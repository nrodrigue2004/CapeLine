# Decisions

One line each, newest at the bottom. Spec = "Capeline build spec".

- 2026-11-13 (nate, in the spec): 22 weeks with no cuts; PvP toggle at level 10 plus the Comet Crater forced zone; accept a Moderate rating; no typed hero names (title and noun wheels); level cap 40.
- 2026-11-16 (nate): start Capeline now; other new games paused. Build milestone after milestone without pausing for Studio checks.
- 2026-11-16 (nate): Capeline is superhero AND anime. Added at launch: (1) a per-hero Comic or Anime effect style plus anime creator pieces (hair, coats, scarves, wraps, glowing eyes), looks only; (2) Awakening, a meter-filled 15 s power-up for every hero (aura, glow, stronger super); (3) a fifth power set, Blade (energy sword dashes, combo strings, a slash-wave super). About +3 weeks: Phase 1 about 25 weeks. All original, nothing copied from real shows.
- 2026-11-16: the spec's "reuse Hive Hold enemy AI and boss framework" can't happen (Hive Hold isn't built); enemies and bosses are written fresh (EnemySim) in Mossbell Stand's plain-data style.
- 2026-11-16: added an Enemy.Delta remote (10 Hz, unreliable, 10 bytes per enemy, only enemies within 450 studs). The spec's remote list had no enemy replication line.
- 2026-11-16: out-of-combat regen 4% of max health per second after 6 s without damage (Config.Stats.regen). The spec has no PvE healing outside abilities, which would leave solo heroes slowly bleeding out between incidents. (tune)
- 2026-11-16: leaps use their own gravity (airGravity 120) so the spec's apex 40 and range 80 give a floaty 1.6 s arc; Roblox's 196 felt like a hop.
- 2026-11-16: speed check uses a travel budget (cap x 1.15 per second, banking 0.5 s) instead of judging each 0.1 s sample alone, so network bursts never trip it. A fail only pulls the hero back; only teleport-sized jumps count as strikes (3 in 10 minutes kicks, no bans).
- 2026-11-16: Zap Bolt and the other basics keep a 0.5 s fire interval (2 per second) though the spec lists cooldown 0; "0" means no skill cooldown.
- 2026-11-16: M1 slice unlocks Flight for everyone (Config.Dev.unlockAllStyles) so the move-and-fight test needs no levels. Turned off in M3 when levels save (spec: Flight at level 3).
- 2026-11-16: M1 default hero is a level 1 Blaster with Volt (basic + Chain Lightning + Dash Strike) until the creator (M9).
- 2026-11-16: world origin is the map centre: map -2,400 to 2,400, hub -800 to 800, Downtown x -750 to -50, z -350 to 350. The Downtown greybox has a fixed seed (the hub is handcrafted in the spec and never changes with the weekly seed).
- 2026-11-16 (nate): enemies are humanoid robots ("robots that look like humans"): metal android bodies with glowing eyes, walking and punching. Spark Drone becomes a jet-boot flyer, Turret a planted heavy sentry android. Still machines only: defeats are sparks, bolts and parts, never blood.
- 2026-11-16: a hero's skills may come from any unlocked set (spec: "Any class can use any set"; the 52-point cap only bites when mixing sets). The primary set (hero.setId) gives the basic attack and the look.
- 2026-11-16: Blade set (abilities 33-40) numbers: Blade Slash 0.65/0.65/1.0 combo, Flash Step 1.4 dash 35, Crescent Wave 1.3 line 50, Rising Blade 1.5 knockup, Thousand Cuts 8 x 0.35 flurry, Parry Stance 1 s counter 2.0, Blade Storm 0.35 per tick r10 4 s, Heaven Splitter super 4.2 line 80 x 14. Unlocks at level 18. (tune)
- 2026-11-16: Awakening numbers: meter 100; +1.5 per basic hit, +4 per skill hit, +8 per super hit, +6 per kill, +0.5 per 1% health lost; 15 s of +20% damage, skill cooldowns 35% faster, supers +30% coeff and half cooldown. Same for every hero; nothing sold touches it. (tune)
- 2026-11-16: supers have 3 ranks: 2 = +10% coeff and the mod pick, 3 = capstone and -8% cooldown. Rank Credits cost is waived (Config.Dev.freeRankCredits) until Credits exist in M3.
- 2026-11-16: Rally Roar gives +10% Power, not "+10% Might" (Might is 0 without gear). Bruiser hits taunt the target 2 s. Loadouts change anywhere out of combat (5 s since damage) until safehouses exist in M4 (superseded by the M4 safehouse rule below).
- 2026-11-16: 4 more team-up combos for the Blade set (Storm Edge, Ice Bloom, Sky Breaker, Void Cut), 10 in all.
- 2026-11-16: melee basics (Punch Combo, Blade Slash) swing every 0.5 s like the ranged basics; their combo strings average about 1.4x a ranged basic's damage for the risk of being close. A level 10 Burglar Bot falls in about 2.1 to 2.3 s to any basic (spec test: 2 to 3 s).
- 2026-11-16: Enemy.Delta grew to 12 bytes per enemy (status flags and facing for the robot bodies); 60 enemies still fit in under 900 bytes.
- 2026-11-16: M3 gear. A Common piece (no bonus stats) puts its whole budget in the main stat; the spec's 70% main share applies when there are bonus stats to take the rest.
- 2026-11-16: upgrade step cost follows the spec formula round(12 x n^1.5 x tier mult): +10 on a Common is 379 (the spec text says 380).
- 2026-11-16: supers rank for 300, 900 and 2,400 Credits (spec Sinks table), not the skill prices.
- 2026-11-16: pity counts the boss in hand: the 5th boss in a row with no Epic is an Epic, the 15th MVP roll in a row with no Legendary is a Legendary (spec test wording). Fixed story rewards (the Act 1 finale Epic) skip the level 20 Epic gate; every other Epic or Legendary above the hero's gate drops one tier lower.
- 2026-11-16: the 5th power set (Blade) gets a 5th Epic set, Edgewalker: 2 pieces Blade damage +8%, 4 pieces Parry Stance ripostes heal 5%. Ironclad's shockwave is 0.3 coeff per 0.5 s for 3 s; Orbitwear's Float heal reaches allies within 12 studs of the lifted target (Float lifts machines).
- 2026-11-16: a respec refunds the Credits its ranks cost into the bank, so trying builds stays free (spec: respec is free).
- 2026-11-16: gadget actives (Grappling Hook 30 s, Smoke Bomb 40 s, Shield Drone 45 s, Decoy 60 s) ship in M3 with the gadget slot; about 6% of drops are gadgets.
- 2026-11-16: server close waits up to 25 s for saves (Roblox allows 30); the spec's 4 s deadline would only drop slow saves. Saves also go 8 s after a level up, drop, gear change, rank or loadout change (one write per burst).
- 2026-11-16: a full stash (150) turns a new drop into Lumen Dust; its style still unlocks.
- 2026-11-16: Profile.Sync sends the full view after gear changes and a small wallet-only view after rewards and spending.
- 2026-11-16: until incidents and bosses exist (M6), nothing in play drops gear or pays Renown; the Dev panel rolls any source and adds Renown, Credits and Dust.
- 2026-11-16: M4 tile hash is FNV-1a (32-bit) over the 4,608-byte tile array, not SHA: Luau has no SHA built in, and the hash only has to match between two servers. Logged as "[CityBuilder] seed N tile hash X" and set as Workspace attributes WorldSeed and WorldHash.
- 2026-11-16: zone rings count tiles out from the hub edge: suburbs 1 to 7 (the edge wobbles by one tile in 4 x 4 blocks so the ring is not a square), farmland 8 to 14, coast 15 and 16. The spec's "suburbs 8 to 14 tiles from the hub edge" would leave rings 1 to 7 empty, so suburbs take the inner half and farmland the outer.
- 2026-11-16: each tile is 2 bytes: zone, variant and rotation as the spec says, plus 4 road bits (local and highway, each direction). Roads run through tile centres (highway every 6 tiles, 30 wide; local every 3, 16 wide) as long strips; each tile splits into 4 lots around its roads and a variant names what stands on each lot (suburbs 12, farmland 10, coast 4, hub filler 6). Rotation turns the lot pattern.
- 2026-11-16: the hub is code-built from fixed seeds (Downtown, the Docks, Old Town, filler tiles between them), so it never changes with the weekly seed. The spec's saved handcrafted hub model can replace it at the M5 kit pass.
- 2026-11-16: 16 safehouses: Downtown 4 on reserved lots (the rest of Downtown is unchanged), Docks 3, Old Town 3, 2 hub parks, 4 outskirts (Northgate, Eastfield, Southmoor, Westbrook) on tiles kept off the road lines. Beacons: Beacon Tower, Downtown East roof, Harbor Crane, Clock Tower, the 3 landmarks, the 4 outskirts safehouse roofs and the Windmill.
- 2026-11-16: the bank, loadout and respawn terminals are ProximityPrompts (key B, gamepad X). The bank is a 3 s hold that moves every on-hand Credit into the bank; the prompt itself is the server check, so there is no Bank.Deposit remote.
- 2026-11-16: loadout changes and respec now need a safehouse (40 studs) or HQ (80 studs of Beacon Tower or the Foundry), or the Quick Swap pass for loadouts; this replaces the M2 "anywhere out of combat" rule. Rank-ups and mods still work anywhere.
- 2026-11-16: respawns: a defeat lands at the nearest visited safehouse (Plaza West is everyone's home); a returning player joins at their chosen safehouse; a new hero lands on the plaza until the M7 opening. Walking onto a safehouse marks it visited and makes it the respawn point if none is chosen yet; the Respawn terminal chooses one.
- 2026-11-16: fast travel: the server checks the beacon, combat and the 20 s cooldown (from departure), the client plays a 3 s camera flight with SKIP, and the server moves the hero when it ends; the hero is untargetable on the way and the destination is streamed in first.
- 2026-11-16: streaming: Roblox has one streaming radius per place (600, the medium preset), lowered by the engine on low-memory phones; the presets' radius numbers stay as targets for the M10 pass. StreamOutBehavior is Opportunistic; hub chunks use the StreamingMesh level of detail so the skyline shows far away; each outskirts tile is one Atomic model.
- 2026-11-16: the world is about 36,900 parts (hub 2,600 greybox, outskirts and landmarks 34,100, base 150): about 16.6 per outskirts tile against the 25 budget. The whole world as data builds in 0.06 s in Lune; parts build 40 tiles a frame after the hub.
- 2026-11-16: the ocean runs 2,000 studs past the map edge at y -2 and can be stood on (PLACEHOLDER); a soft push back at the map edge comes with the M8 anti-exploit pass.
- 2026-11-16: profile version 2 adds per-character beacons and safehouses bit masks; a version 1 save keeps its last safehouse as visited.
- 2026-11-16: M5 the 220 wreckable hub buildings are every Downtown building (92), the 6 Docks warehouses and 122 Old Town houses picked by a fixed hash; outskirts houses, the Foundry, cranes and the Clock Tower crack but never fall. HP by height: up to 30 studs small (6,000), up to 150 medium (10,000), taller towers 14,000.
- 2026-11-16: M5 the server keeps only BuildingState (HP and 2-bit state per building); clients split each building with the shared, seeded Chunker (10 to 40 chunks in floor rows) and draw Damaged, collapse and rubble themselves, so every device drops the same chunks.
- 2026-11-16: M5 "at most 3 collapses at once" means 3 collapses started within the 6 s collapse animation; a 4th is cut to Damaged at 1 HP.
- 2026-11-16: M5 the snapshot is sent when the client asks (BuildingSync, once its listeners are up) so a joining player never misses a diff; diffs batch at 2 Hz with drone jobs. BuildingImpact (center, radius, crater or cracks) goes to players within 300 studs, at most 6 a second per server.
- 2026-11-16: M5 structure damage: an ability's structDmg, or 3,000 for a super without one, where the ability lands (bolts, chains, dashes, cones, arcs, novas, lines; leaps and throws on landing). Buffs, walls, ramps and fields never damage buildings.
- 2026-11-16: M5 a heavy hit is a hit of 2.5x Power or more (Combat.heavyCoeff): it flips vehicles within 10 studs of the target and, if the robot is within 20 studs of a wall facing away from the hero, knocks it 26 studs at the wall and the wall turns Damaged (3 per player per 10 s).
- 2026-11-16: M5 collapsed buildings stop colliding and block no shots on the server until repaired; enemy heights drop to the rubble heap (12 studs at most).
- 2026-11-16: M5 40 hub vehicles are anchored part models placed from fixed street slots; a flip sets the landed pose on the server at once and clients play the 1 s arc from the shared VehiclePose maths, so no physics is replicated.
- 2026-11-16: M5 citizens are server data like the robots (CitizenSim on the sidewalk corner graph, 10 Hz) sent to each player within 250 studs at 4 Hz in a 9-byte-a-citizen unreliable Citizen.Delta (not in the spec's remote list). Danger comes from the 75% warning, collapses and every structural hit; shelters are the Downtown and Old Town safehouses and the Beacon Tower door.
- 2026-11-16: M5 one shared client debris budget (DebrisBudget, the preset's debris count) covers robot defeat bolts, falling chunks and vehicle sparks; dust clouds are limited by the preset's emitter count.
- 2026-11-16: M6 zone level is the hero's own level raised to the area floor (hub 1, suburbs 5, farmland 12, coast 20, Comet Crater 10, Old Quarry 20, cap 40); events and the boss use the median level of the heroes in the server, raised the same way.
- 2026-11-16: M6 rescues and the Contract Board use ProximityPrompts (hold B / gamepad X) as the server's check, so Rescue.Pick and Rescue.Drop have no remotes; carrying a citizen into a drop circle is the drop. Citizens can never be hurt: a defeated carrier sets them down where they were.
- 2026-11-16: M6 the M1 ambient robot spawner is off (EnemyService.populate false): incidents, contracts, events and the boss bring every robot. DevPanel's Spawner button turns it back on for load tests.
- 2026-11-16: M6 the spec has no random event reward row: each participant gets 60 + 4 x L Credits and 80 + 6 x L Renown (times the friend and Legacy bonus) plus one "event" gear roll; in the Meteor shower the roll is the shard itself. Participants are everyone who hit its robots, carried, held, hit the train or grabbed a shard, plus anyone within 150 studs when it clears. A failed event pays nothing.
- 2026-11-16: M6 events happen where they make sense: Meteor shower at the plaza, Tidal surge at the Docks, Blackout in Old Town, Train hijack on a Downtown tram line, Robot rampage and Vault breach near a random player. Never in a mission pocket (y 2,000 and up).
- 2026-11-16: M6 boss cosmetics: Scrapjaw Visor (head, Rustjaw Prime) and Maw Shard Fins (back, Lumen Maw), each with a shiny version at 1 in 20 (tier "Boss" styles). Style chance by tier: MVP 100%, Contributor 50%, Participant 20%. The Rogues File page goes to every tier on a defeat.
- 2026-11-16: M6 tiers: MVP top 3, Contributor top 50%, Participant at 10% of the median score or more; anyone below that who scored at all gets the 25% floor (late joiners). A retreat pays 25% of the tier's Credits only.
- 2026-11-16: M6 support score: shield points and healing given to allies count 1 for 1 (HeroService.Supported), a rescue during the fight counts 400 and a revive 800 (M8); breaking a cannon or generator adds 1,000 on top of the damage done to it.
- 2026-11-16: M6 Raid prep: each Raid prep incident cleared in the last 5 minutes before a boss takes 3% off its HP, at most 15%.
- 2026-11-16: M6 schedule: a fresh server's first boss lands after 10 minutes; a player with stats.bossesJoined = 0 pulls the next boss to at most 6 minutes after they load (the "within 8 minutes" check). bossesJoined counts once per fight in BossService, not per drop.
- 2026-11-16: M6 arenas: Rustjaw Prime lands on the street just south of the plaza and walks toward the crowd within 220 studs of it; the Lumen Maw rises in the Old Quarry and only turns. Both bosses are client-drawn (WorldFx) from Boss.State; the server only has hit targets (EnemyService.addTarget).
- 2026-11-16: M6 the Contract Board is a 4th terminal at every safehouse. Contracts open at level 4, max 3 accepted, one of each type. A saved contract keeps its seed and the board it came from, so a rejoin rebuilds the same sites. The escort truck follows a 16-leg street path; its progress is scaled to the spec's 600 studs. A Stakeout Sneak Bot beaten before its photo is replaced nearby.
- 2026-11-16: M6 remotes beyond the spec's list: ContractBoard (the board screen) and BossAttack (telegraphs and strikes for the effects). Incident.Update is one batched array per player at 2 Hz.
- 2026-11-16: M6 the day and night is client-side lighting: the server sends only the cycle start (World.Clock on join and every 60 s, plus the Workspace attribute); night gets a cool ambient so the city stays readable. Villain incidents and contracts are 30% likelier at night; nothing is locked to night.
- 2026-11-16: tooling: luau-lsp 1.32.0 (MIT) now downloads fine; `luau-lsp analyze` with the 1.32.0 Roblox definitions runs as an extra check (its nonstrict warnings about optional values are noise; read new errors in changed files).
